# The website itself

**Pattern 002 · Category: architecture**

**Created: 2026-10-22T17:05:21Z**

> *“Why write articles when you can build a platform to publish them?”*

## Problem

You have six ideas for a small blog and a weekend to spare.

## Solution

Before writing the first article, design the perfect publishing platform. Add an author dashboard, custom CMS, database, authentication, deployment pipeline, and monitoring. Keep improving the platform whenever content creation feels difficult.

## Benefits

- No vendor lock-in.
- Unlimited opportunities for refactoring.
- Impressive infrastructure diagrams and zero editorial pressure.

## Success metric

The publishing platform needs more documentation than the articles it hosts.

## The ultimate achievement

The site launches with a stunning homepage and an empty catalog.
