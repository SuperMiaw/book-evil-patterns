# The application collection

**Pattern 007 · Category: maintenance**

**Created: 2026-10-22T17:05:21Z**

**Last modified: 2026-10-22T17:19:12Z**

> *“The best software is software you can maintain forever.”*

## Problem

You have a problem.

Fortunately, you are a developer. Why waste time looking for an existing solution when you can build one perfectly tailored to your needs?

One application solves one problem. Another solves another. Soon, you have an entire collection of perfectly tailored tools.

There is only one minor inconvenience: software requires maintenance.

## Solution

Build more applications.

Give every new requirement its own solution, dependencies, and upgrade cycle.

As their developer, you control their architecture. As their user, you experience every migration, breaking change, and unexpected regression firsthand.

Eventually, updating your applications becomes a regular activity. The time they save can be reinvested directly into maintaining them.

## Benefits

- Every problem has a dedicated solution.
- You gain experience maintaining an increasingly diverse software ecosystem.
- You never run out of personal development projects.
- Your technical skills never become obsolete.

## Success metric

You spend more time maintaining your applications than actually using them.

## The ultimate achievement

You build an application to keep all your applications up to date.

It also needs updates.
