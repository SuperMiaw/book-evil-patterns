# The technical autobiography

**Pattern 008 · Category: documentation**

**Created: 2026-10-22T17:09:57Z**

**Last modified: 2026-10-22T17:20:29Z**

> *“Every bug deserves a chapter.”*

## Problem

You have been developing software for years.

Along the way, you have encountered mysterious bugs, questionable decisions, and solutions that seemed perfectly reasonable at the time.

Some experiences taught you valuable lessons.

Others taught you why the previous lessons were wrong.

Unfortunately, most of this knowledge exists only in your memory. Clearly, it deserves to be preserved.

## Solution

Document your experiences.

Start with a few technical notes explaining what went wrong and what you learned.

But every incident needs context. A workaround requires architectural history. Architectural history requires explaining the decisions that led to it.

Soon, a note becomes an article. An article becomes a series. A series becomes a collection of lessons learned.

Eventually, your technical decisions form a coherent narrative, complete with recurring characters, unexpected plot twists, and occasional tragedies.

There is only one logical next step.

Write a book.

## Benefits

- Every mistake becomes valuable source material.
- Every refactoring provides a new chapter.
- Your abandoned projects finally have a purpose.
- Documenting your experiences creates new experiences worth documenting.

## Success metric

You spend more time explaining why you built your software than building it.

## The ultimate achievement

You started documenting your software.

You ended up documenting yourself.
